# Nordion photographic direction

Full-width photographs, minimum viewport-height chapters (100vh / 100svh), graphite overlays, ivory type and copper accents. Sections grow when translated text, zoom or small screens require more room. No clipped content or forced scroll snapping.

## Supplied photographs

- **Portrait:** the only landscape source. The subject occupies the left/center, leaving the right side for the hero headline. Desktop keeps the face on the left; mobile preserves the face above a darker text area. Also used for the about chapter, without implying the model is a real team member.
- **Architecture:** vertical converging lines communicate international scale. Center crop retains the towers; subdued saturation and a graphite overlay unify the cool glass with the warm palette. The source is Frankfurt, so it is atmospheric international imagery, not a claim to depict Brazil.
- **Planning:** neutral gray negative space above the desk supports process headings; lower framing preserves collaboration in the background. Any incidental numbers belong to the supplied photograph and are not company statistics.
- **Team:** faces and tablet concentrate toward the center/right. Text and translucent panels sit over darkened areas; the crop changes on mobile to retain the working relationship.
- **Founder:** vertical image, warm wood and architectural yellow. A right-biased crop preserves the face and laptop; the left gradient supports text. Used for audience and the explicitly fictional scenario, not a testimonial.

Originals remain unchanged in Downloads. Local WebP derivatives at 960 and 1920 px reduce transfer size. CSS handles framing, saturation and overlays. No external image requests.

## Motion and interaction

Sections reveal once on entry. Embla services carousel supports touch, arrow keys, previous/next controls and direct slide selection; no automatic advancement. React Three Fiber renders a copper orbital globe in the Brazil chapter, loaded only near the viewport. Reduced motion uses a static orbital illustration; rendering errors retain the fallback. No geographic statistics are invented.
